DroPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

DroPilot is a musical tool for guitarists designed to operate mostly offline. This policy explains what information is processed, for what purposes, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

Personal data and user accounts

✅

DroPilot does not require an account or registration. We do not collect or store your name, email address, passwords, or contacts.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create while using the app—saved chord progressions, folders, sound preferences, tuning presets, language, and visual theme—is stored exclusively on your device using standard local storage (SharedPreferences).

Your progressions and settings are not transmitted to custom servers and are not accessible to the developer.

Microphone permission

The tuner feature requests access to the device microphone. This permission is used solely to:

  • Detect the acoustic pitch of guitar notes in real time.
  • Display the nearest musical note and tuning offset on screen.

Microphone audio is not recorded, not saved to files, and not transmitted under any circumstances. Audio calculations occur entirely in your device’s volatile memory.

No in-app advertising

DroPilot does not display advertisements inside the application (no banner ads, no interstitial popups, and no rewarded videos). Current releases across Android, iOS, and Windows are completely ad-free.

Ad campaign measurement & optimization (Meta App Events)

On Android, DroPilot integrates the Meta App Events SDK (Meta Platforms, Inc.) to measure the effectiveness of our external advertising campaigns (on Facebook and Instagram) and optimize them for musicians interested in DroPilot Pro.

Mandatory prior consent: Data transmission to Meta is completely disabled by default and only activates if you give explicit consent through the dialog shown inside the app.

If you choose to allow measurement, the following pseudonymous data may be shared with Meta:

  • Device identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) and technical identifiers generated by the SDK.
  • App usage events: App launch and session activation.
  • Purchase funnel events: Viewing the DroPilot Pro screen (ViewContent), tapping to initiate checkout (InitiatedCheckout), and successful purchase confirmation (Purchase), including currency and amount paid.

Control and revocation: You can decline measurement by choosing “Don't allow” on the initial prompt, or revoke consent anytime in Settings ➔ Privacy & Measurement by turning off the Meta campaign measurement toggle. Revoking consent immediately stops Advertising ID collection and blocks any future event transmission to Meta, even after relaunching the application.

For further details on how Meta processes information, see the Meta Privacy Policy .

Product analytics (Firebase Analytics)

DroPilot uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) as an internal product analytics tool to understand general feature usage and diagnose technical errors.

Firebase collects first-party app-instance identifiers and aggregated functional events (such as tools opened, transaction errors, or paywall views). It does not collect your personal identity and is not shared with external ad networks for cross-app profiling.

For more information, visit the Google Privacy Policy .

In-app purchases and payments

DroPilot offers an optional “DroPilot Pro” one-time purchase to unlock all advanced features permanently. Payments are handled exclusively by your platform’s official app store:

  • Android: Google Play Billing (Google LLC).
  • iOS / macOS: StoreKit (Apple Inc.).
  • Windows: Microsoft Store (Microsoft Corporation).

The developer of DroPilot does not have access to and does not store your payment methods, card numbers, or billing addresses. We only receive technical confirmation from the store to validate your license and grant lifetime Pro access.

Your rights and how to exercise them

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR (DPA 2018), you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction regarding your data:

  • Revoking ad measurement consent: You can revoke consent directly and instantly within the app by going to Settings ➔ Privacy & Measurement.
  • Resetting or deleting your Advertising ID: You can delete or reset your device identifier anytime in your Android device settings (Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Delete advertising ID), which decouples your device from historical data in advertising networks.
  • Privacy inquiries: Because DroPilot does not manage user accounts or emails, if you have any inquiries regarding data processing, please contact:
    pniveiroj@gmail.com

Children’s privacy

DroPilot is an educational music tool and is not knowingly directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect technical enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
