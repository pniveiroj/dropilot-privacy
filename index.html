DroPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

DroPilot is an offline-first musical tool for guitarists. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

Personal data

✅

DroPilot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No user account, email address, or registration is required to use the application.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create—saved chord progressions, folders, language preferences, theme, and audio settings—is stored only on your device using standard local storage (SharedPreferences).

This information is never sent to external servers, is not shared with third parties, and is not accessible to the developer.

Microphone permission

The tuner feature may request microphone access. It is used solely to:

  • Detect the acoustic pitch of your instrument in real time.
  • Display the nearest musical note and its tuning offset on screen.

Audio captured by the microphone is never recorded, never stored in files, and never transmitted over the network. Processing happens entirely in your device's volatile memory.

In-app purchases

DroPilot offers an optional “DroPilot Pro” one-time purchase to permanently unlock all advanced features. Transactions are processed directly by your platform’s official store:

  • Android: Google Play Billing (Google LLC).
  • iOS / macOS: StoreKit (Apple Inc.).
  • Windows: Microsoft Store (Microsoft Corporation).

The developer of DroPilot does not have access to and does not store your payment methods, card details, or billing addresses. We only receive technical confirmation from the store to validate your lifetime license.

Third-party services & advertising

DroPilot does not display advertisements and does not integrate ad networks, social trackers, or third-party marketing SDKs that collect usage data, advertising identifiers, or behavioural profiles.

Children’s privacy

DroPilot is an educational music tool, is not directed specifically at children under 13, and does not collect data from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change in the future, this page will be updated with a new revision date. Please check back occasionally.
