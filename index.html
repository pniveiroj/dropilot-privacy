DroPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

DroPilot is an offline-first musical tool for guitarists. This policy explains what technical information is processed, the applicable legal bases, how the integration with Firebase and Google Ads operates, and the practical methods available to exercise your privacy rights.

1. Personal Data & User Account

✅

DroPilot does not require or request user registration. We do not collect or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords. The application can be used without creating a user account.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Musical data and application settings configured by the user—saved chord progressions, favorite folders, custom tunings, audio settings, language, and visual theme—are stored exclusively on your device using standard operating system local storage (SharedPreferences).

These files and configurations are never uploaded to proprietary servers of the developer and reside strictly within your device's local memory.

3. Microphone Permission (Tuner)

The tuner feature requests microphone access solely to analyze acoustic audio frequencies in real time and display the detected musical pitch and tuning variance on screen.

Microphone audio is never recorded, never saved to persistent storage, and never transmitted across the internet. Processing takes place in real time within your device's volatile memory (RAM) while using the tuner and is immediately discarded.

4. No In-App Advertising

DroPilot does not display advertisements within the application (no banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, or rewarded video ads). The user experience is entirely free from embedded advertising networks.

5. Product Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

On Android and iOS versions, DroPilot integrates Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google LLC) to monitor functional tool usage and evaluate the conversion funnel.

Through this service, pseudonymous installation identifiers (app-instance ID) and specific in-app navigation events are processed:

  • Application startup (app_open).
  • Pro paywall presentation and its trigger source (pro_paywall_shown).
  • Purchase or restore button taps (pro_purchase_tap, pro_restore_tap).
  • Transaction status outcomes (pro_purchase_success or pro_purchase_fail).
  • Technical user properties for aggregate cohort segmentation: is_pro and access_tier.

6. Advertising Identifier (AD_ID) & Google Ads Measurement

In the release build for Android, Google Play Services Measurement libraries bundle the Android Advertising Identifier permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

The Firebase project is linked with the Google Ads advertising platform. When a user purchases DroPilot Pro or completes a conversion action, conversion events and associated technical identifiers (such as the AD_ID, if available and enabled on the device) are communicated to Google Ads.

This communication is intended to assess the performance of user acquisition campaigns across Google's advertising network and attribute purchases to originating ads. Consequently, Google may link these app interactions with advertising profiles or user Google Accounts in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

7. DroPilot Pro Purchase & Billing

DroPilot offers an optional «DroPilot Pro» purchase as a one-time permanent payment (non-consumable product, identifier pro_unlock) to permanently unlock the complete voicings library and advanced tools. It is not a recurring subscription and does not generate ongoing charges.

Payment collection and transaction processing are managed exclusively by the official app store for your device:

  • Android: Google Play Billing (Google LLC).
  • iOS / macOS: StoreKit (Apple Inc.).
  • Windows: Microsoft Store (Microsoft Corporation).

The developer of DroPilot does not have access to payment card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses. The developer only receives a technical transaction receipt token from the platform store to validate purchase authenticity and unlock Pro features.

8. Legal Bases for Processing & Consent Status (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR 2016/679 and UK GDPR), the data processing activities described are carried out under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Applicable to providing the app's musical features and activating the DroPilot Pro license requested by the user.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Applicable to basic aggregate technical measurement necessary for software maintenance.
  • Consent & current technical status (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Under European privacy regulations (ePrivacy Directive and GDPR), terminal equipment identifier access (such as the AD_ID) and transmission to Google Ads for ad attribution require prior, informed, and unambiguous consent.

Transparency Notice on Consent: The application does not currently feature an in-app consent prompt banner nor does it transmit configuration parameters via Google Consent Mode v2. Operating system controls (such as deleting the AD_ID) allow users to restrict tracking, but do not on their own constitute an in-app affirmative consent collection mechanism under European regulatory standards. This technical implementation is currently undergoing legal review and future architectural adjustment.

9. Service Providers & International Data Transfers

Analytics, attribution, and payment infrastructure services are provided by Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA).

The operation of these services involves data processing on servers outside the European Economic Area. These international transfers are safeguarded under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), under which Google LLC is formally certified with the U.S. Department of Commerce, as well as European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).

10. Data Retention Periods

  • On-Device Local Data: Retained indefinitely on the device until the user clears application storage or uninstalls the app.
  • Individual Event Data in Firebase Analytics: User-level and event records linked to installation identifiers are retained according to the period configured in Google Analytics settings (standard of 2 months or 14 months), after which Google automatically deletes these individual records.
  • Aggregated Statistical Reports: Consolidated aggregate metric reports generated by Google Analytics (overall session counts, total purchases, or screen views dissociated from individual identifiers) are retained for historical analysis without automatic expiration.

11. Information Security

Network transmissions to Google Play and Firebase are encrypted using standard HTTPS / TLS protocols. Locally, application data is isolated within operating system sandboxed environments. The application does not integrate remote crash reporting libraries (no Crashlytics or similar third-party tools); internal diagnostic logs are saved to a strictly local file within device storage.

12. Data Subject Rights & Deletion Requests

Under the GDPR, users hold rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection. To exercise them, distinct operational environments must be distinguished:

A. Deleting Local Device Data:

You can immediately erase all saved progressions and settings without developer intervention:

  • On Android: navigate to Settings ➔ Apps ➔ DroPilot ➔ Storage ➔ Clear Storage / Clear Data (or uninstall the app).
  • On iOS / macOS / Windows: uninstall the application from your device.

B. Halting Future Collection & Advertising ID (AD_ID) Reset:

  • Delete Advertising ID: On Android, go to Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Delete advertising ID. This deletes the identifier from the device so future interactions cannot be linked to your prior advertising profile. However, this action does not retroactively delete historic records previously transmitted to Google.
  • Stopping Future Telemetry: Uninstalling the application completely halts the transmission of any new events from that device to Firebase.

C. Erasure Requests for Historical Firebase Logs:

Previously logged records remain on Google servers until the retention window expires or a deletion request is processed:

  • You may submit an erasure request by writing to pniveiroj@gmail.com.
  • Essential Technical Limitation: Because DroPilot does not manage user accounts, emails, or personal names, records stored in Firebase are strictly pseudonymous (tied to a randomized app-instance ID). The developer cannot locate or delete records corresponding to an individual person unless the user provides the specific technical installation identifier of their app (Firebase App Instance ID).

13. Children’s Privacy

DroPilot is an educational music tool and is not knowingly directed at children under 13, nor does it knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
