DroPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

DroPilot is an offline-first musical tool for guitarists. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

Personal data

✅

DroPilot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account, email address, or registration is required to use the app.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create—saved chord progressions, folders, language preferences, theme, and other settings—is stored only on your device using Android’s standard local storage (SharedPreferences).

This information is never sent to external servers, is not shared with third parties, and is not accessible to the developer.

Microphone permission

The tuner feature may request microphone access. It is used solely to:

  • Detect the pitch of the sound in real time.
  • Show the nearest note and how far it is from being in tune.

Audio from the microphone is not recorded, not stored, and not transmitted. Processing happens entirely on your device.

In-app purchases

DroPilot offers an optional “DroPilot Pro” purchase to unlock advanced features. The transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy .

The DroPilot developer does not have access to your payment details—only whether the purchase completed successfully.

Third-party services

DroPilot does not integrate analytics, advertising, social networks, or any third-party SDKs that collect usage or behavioural data.

Children

The app is not directed specifically at children under 13 and does not collect data from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date. Please check back occasionally.
